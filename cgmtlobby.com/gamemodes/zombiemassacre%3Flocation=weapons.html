Arsenal of the Apocalypse

A variety of weapons are available to fight back the zombie hordes. Each has its strengths and can be upgraded for maximum effectiveness.

PISTOLS

.357 MAGNUM

Powerful revolver that can take down basic zombies with one headshot. Slow reload but great accuracy.

DUAL PISTOLS

Double the firepower with two automatic pistols. Great for crowd control but burns through ammo quickly.

SHOTGUNS

PUMP SHOTGUN

Classic zombie-slaying weapon. Devastating at close range and can hit multiple targets.

AUTO SHOTGUN

Fully automatic shotgun with a high rate of fire. Perfect for clearing rooms but expensive to maintain.

HEAVY WEAPONS

M249 SAW

Light machine gun with huge magazine capacity. Excellent for holding chokepoints.

GRENADE LAUNCHER

Launches explosive grenades that can clear entire groups. Be careful of splash damage!
