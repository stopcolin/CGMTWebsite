HOW MUCH GMC CAN YOU EARN?

Let's not forget that one of the many reasons to play gamemodes (other than having fun) is to earn GMC. GMC is used to spend on Lobby items and upgrades. It's a reward for playing our games - how nice of us!

So, exactly how much GMC can you earn while playing this gamemode?

Each level (not work!) you earn GMC based on the amount of bananas you collected compared to everyone else. The more bananas, the higher rank you get at the end.

1st Place - 150 GMC
2nd Place - 100 GMC
3rd Place - 50 GMC
Just for Playing - 25 GMC

Completing the level will gain you 25 bonus GMC.
