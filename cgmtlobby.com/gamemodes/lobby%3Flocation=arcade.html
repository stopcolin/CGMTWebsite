Games for all to enjoy.

At the arcade, we have all sorts of different games you can play for your enjoyment. From Lua-based games to in-game flash games, the multi-story arcade is just the place to rack up some GMC while staying in the lobby.

Blockles

Move the different shaped blocks into the correct fashion as they travel downward. Line them up perfectly to clear a horizontal row, giving you points which then turn into GMC. The longer you last and the more points you rank up, the more GMC you'll get from playing this definitely not a Tetris clone game.

Multiplayer Trivia

Compete against eight other players to see who's the smartest by testing their knowledge on, well, just about anything. Multiple questions on all different types of subjects will satisfy your trivia needs. Oh and the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd winners in each trivia match get a nice GMC reward!

Tic-tac-toe

A two player game that seemingly never has a winner (well, if each person is somewhat smart). Simply find a buddy and play together to pass the time.

Flash Arcades

Try out popular (community suggested) flash games while in-game. All hosted by our server, you can simply just walk up and play. Well known flash games such as Fancy Pants Adventure, Portal 2D, Doom, N, Last Stand, Mario 63, flOw, and much much more! Each flash game has its own cabinet with custom designed marquee and side art.

Please note that this is a parody game and any similarities to existing games are purely coincidental.
