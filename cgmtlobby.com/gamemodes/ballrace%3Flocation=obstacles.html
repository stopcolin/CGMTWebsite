Here you'll find a list of all the obstacles that are known to be in Ball Race. Obstacles are just one element of the puzzles to Ball Race. They can be your friend or enemy based on how you use them.

CRATE

First appeared in: Sky World

A simple box that weights quite a bit and can be hard to push. Can be spawned from above.

BUMPERS

First appeared in: Grass World

These are everyone's friend. Once you collide with them, they fire you back based on your velocity. Even the slightest touch can send you into a frenzy of bumper hell.

ROCKS

First appeared in: Grass World

Falling rocks from above. Tricky to get past as they can knock your ball right off course.

CHEMICALS

First appeared in: Grass World

A nasty pool of chemicals just ready to burst your orb. Stay clear away from this toxic goo or you'll be wasted trash.

REPELLER

First appeared in: Memories

A magnetic repeller that pushes your orb away from itself. Useful to jump with, but be careful as they can deal more of a push than a normal bumper.

ATTRACTOR

First appeared in: Memories

Just like the repeller, this is a magnetic pole. However these will pull your orb towards themselves. Be careful as if you get too close to their pole - you'll pop.

MAGICAL BLOCK

First appeared in: Memories

These invisible blocks will only appear when you get near them. Can be quite the tricky devils when you're low on time.

EXPLOSIVE CRATE

First appeared in: Paradise

Just like a normal crate, only slightly more explosive.

WINDMILL

First appeared in: Khromidro

A windmill haiku: Round and round it spins/Gusts of wind will make you fall/Do not become sad

BLACK GOO

Coming in: Metal World

Black goo left behind by the unfriendly Ball Race god to annoy you. Once you roll over this your orb will move slowly until its effect as weared off.

MARBLES

Coming in: Metal World

Stay clear of these seemly non-moving marbles. Get too close to them and they'll ram you right off - then go slowly back to their original position.
