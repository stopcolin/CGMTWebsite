Shop far and wide.

In the Lobby, we offer tons of different stores for you to spend your hard earned GMC on. Anything from suite furniture, upgrades for weapons, or even oddities - we've got you covered.

Below you'll find a menu full of all our different types of stores for you to navigate and learn more about.

Decoration Appearance Gamemodes Holidays Other Upcoming

Hat Store

Location: EPlaza

Be proud and wear the hat of your choice. Select from a wide variety of hats such as a top hat, fedora, afro, sombrero, headcrab, aviators, and much much more (34 hats and rising)! Once you buy a hat, simply visit your suite closet to wear it.

All cosmetics, such as hats, transfer for every gamemode the tower supports. So you can look classy with your favorite hat no matter what!

Player Model Store

Location: EPlaza

Buy all types of player models for your use. Once you buy a player model, it works just like an inventory item. Either right click on it and use it, or use tab to select the purchased model. Your player model is also shared across all GMT servers.
