GMod Tower: Lobby 2 is Back Online!

F
By Frontez
May 17, 2025

We’re excited to announce that GMod Tower: Lobby 2 is back and alive once again! The server is now online and fully operational, complete with all gamemodes available and ready for you to enjoy.

Please note: You may still encounter a bug or two as we continue polishing things up. If you do, we’d really appreciate it if you could report them to us so we can fix them quickly!

We’re also working on adding new gamemodes and introducing fresh features in the near future — so stay tuned for more updates!

Joining info can be found in the information channel on discord.

Hop in, bring your friends, and let's make some memories. See you in the Tower!
